Lock-In Trivia

Trivia on anything, written on the spot.

Name any topic — Formula 1, Ancient Rome, 90s one-hit wonders — and AI writes a brand-new question pack in seconds. Play solo, chase the Daily Challenge, or go head-to-head.

iPhone · Android · Free to play

01 Any topic

You name it. The AI writes it.

Type any subject, or pick an arena — Movies, Sports, History, Music, Science, Geography, Gaming or Pop Culture — and get a fresh pack built for that run. No two runs are ever the same.

  • Four difficulties, from Easy to Expert
  • Anywhere from 3 to 20 questions, on the timer you choose
  • Multiple choice, true or false, fill in the blank, short answer and matching
02 Three ways to play

Solo, daily, or head-to-head.

Every mode feeds the same progression, so every game counts.

  • Solo Run — your topic, your pace
  • Daily Challenge — one shared pack a day, with a leaderboard and streaks
  • Multiplayer — 1v1 duels or rooms of up to four players
03 Scoring

Beat the clock. Stack the bonus.

Every correct answer banks 100 points — the bonuses are where runs are won.

  • Speed — answer with time to spare
  • Streaks — keep the right answers coming
  • Question type — extra points for fill-ins, short answers and matching
04 Play together

Matched on what you actually love.

Pick the themes you enjoy and Random Opponent pairs you with live players who share at least one — or invite friends straight into a room with a six-character code.

  • Friend codes, friend requests and room invites
  • Live lobbies and a real-time scoreboard
  • A notification when you’re invited or a match is ready
05 Progression

Keep what you earn.

Every game earns XP towards your next level, and milestones unlock achievements in your vault.

  • Levels, player stats and your recent runs
  • 18 achievements to unlock
  • Daily streaks you won’t want to break
Also inside

Learn as you play

Every answer comes with a short explanation, right or wrong.

Play as a guest

Your soundtrack

Choose your menu and in-game music, with sound effects that make every answer land.

Pick your avatar

Six avatars to choose from, shown on your profile and in every lobby.
