AutoGarage

AI-powered car ownership intelligence: health, maintenance, costs and next steps for your vehicles.

AutoGarage keeps everything about your cars in one place. It forecasts your next services and what they'll cost, tracks what every kilometre really costs you, reads and explains trouble codes from an OBD-II adapter, and answers questions with an AI assistant that knows your vehicle.

iPhone · Android · Free, with Plus and Pro plans

See features See plans
Features

Know your car. Plan what's next.

Smart garage

Add a car by decoding its VIN, scanning a barcode or picking it from the EPA catalogue, and get its official specs and MPG. Give it a custom paint colour and photos, and keep all your vehicles side by side.

Maintenance forecasting

A data-driven service schedule with reminders, a service history that keeps parts and receipts, and cost forecasts for the next 3, 6 and 12 months.

Running costs

Cost per km or mile, a fuel outlook based on your commute and local pump prices, spending by category and over time, and a 5-year cost of ownership.

AutoGarage AI

Chat with an assistant that knows your vehicle. Photograph a warning light to find out what it means, diagnose a symptom, scan a receipt, check a repair quote or check your tires.

OBD-II diagnostics

Connect a Bluetooth or Wi-Fi ELM327 adapter to read trouble codes and see what they mean, check emissions readiness and watch live gauges. No adapter yet? Try the demo car.

Health intelligence

Battery, charging, fuel-mixture and cooling trends, plus a predicted timeline of the maintenance coming up.

Market value

Trade-in, private-sale and dealer estimates for your car, with a curve showing its value over the next 5 years.

PDF reports

A vehicle history report to share when you sell, and an ownership cost report that shows what the car has cost you.

Nearby mechanics

Find top-rated shops near you, ranked by rating and distance.

Glovebox

Keep your car's documents in the app, with automatic reminders when they're due for renewal.

Family sharing

Share a car with family members, and choose whether each person can edit it or only view it.
