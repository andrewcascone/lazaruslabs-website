Features

Know your car. Plan what’s next.

Add a car by decoding its VIN, scanning a barcode or picking it from the EPA catalogue, and AutoGarage fills in its official specs and fuel economy for you.

  • All your vehicles, side by side — each with its own history and plan.
  • Make it yours — a custom paint colour and your own photos.
  • Share with family — choose whether each person can edit or only view.

A data-driven service schedule shows what needs attention, what’s coming up and roughly what each job will cost.

  • Reminders before every upcoming service
  • A service history that keeps parts and receipts
  • Cost forecasts for the next 3, 6 and 12 months

Fuel, maintenance, repairs and financing roll up into one honest number — and AutoGarage shows you where it’s heading.

  • Cost per km or mile, for every car
  • A fuel outlook based on your commute and local pump prices
  • Spending by category, and a 5-year cost of ownership

Ask anything about your vehicle and get answers grounded in its own records — with what’s known, what’s estimated and when to see a mechanic made clear.

  • Photograph a warning light to find out what it means
  • Describe a symptom and get the likely causes
  • Scan a receipt, check a repair quote or check your tires

Connect a Bluetooth or Wi-Fi ELM327 adapter to see exactly what your car is reporting, in plain language.

  • Trouble codes, explained and prioritised
  • Emissions readiness and live gauges
  • No adapter yet? Try the demo car.

Track your car’s market value and keep its paperwork in one place, ready for the day you sell.

  • Trade-in, private-sale and dealer estimates, with a 5-year value curve
  • A glovebox for documents, with reminders before they expire
  • PDF vehicle history and ownership cost reports to share
