Golf Caddie+

Your caddie. Your coach. Your card.

Golf Caddie+ is a golf GPS, scorecard and AI coach in one app. Get distances to the green and hazards, keep score and track every shot, and get coaching built around your game. The Apple Watch companion measures distances on its own, so your phone can stay in the bag.

See features See plans
Features

Everything you need, from the first tee to the 19th hole.

GPS distances

Distances to the front, centre and back of the green, and to hazards.

Course finder

Find courses near you or by name, with course information at a glance.

Scorecard & stats

Keep score, track your shots and see the statistics behind your game.

On-device swing analysis

Analyse your swing with your camera. Analysis runs on your iPhone, and your videos never leave it.

AI coach

Chat with an AI coach, get daily tips and follow personalised practice plans.

Scorecard scanning

Photograph a paper scorecard to add a course that isn't in the app yet.

Apple Watch app

Distances on your wrist, a watch-face complication, and rounds saved to Apple Health as golf workouts.

Privacy

Built to respect your data.

  • Swing videos stay on your phoneSwing analysis runs on your device. Videos are never uploaded.
  • AI only with your permissionNothing is sent to our AI provider until you allow it, and you can turn it off at any time.
  • No ads, no trackingNo ad networks, no tracking across other apps, and we never sell your data.
