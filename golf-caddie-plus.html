Golf Caddie+

Your caddie. Your coach. Your card.

GPS distances, club advice and an AI coach built around your game — with an Apple Watch app that measures distances on its own, so your phone can stay in the bag.

Get it onGoogle Play Coming soon to theApp Store

iPhone · Apple Watch · Android · Free, with Premium and Pro

01 Your caddie

The distance, and the club to hit.

Satellite hole views give you distances to the front, centre and back of the green and to every hazard — and your caddie suggests the club for the shot in front of you.

  • Plays-like yardage that accounts for wind and air
  • Distances to bunkers, water and every hazard in play
  • Course finder — courses near you or by name
02 Scorecard & stats

Keep score. See the story behind it.

Track every shot and watch the statistics behind your game take shape, round after round.

  • Fairways, greens in regulation, putts and driving distance
  • Scoring trends across your last 5, 10 or all rounds
  • Scan a paper scorecard to add a course that isn’t in the app yet
03 Swing analysis

Film a swing. See what changed.

Rotation, tempo and balance measured frame by frame — and the analysis runs entirely on your phone, so your videos never leave it.

  • A score for every swing, and your biggest opportunity
  • What to work on, in the order it costs you shots
  • Drills to fix it, from your coach
04 AI coach

A coach that knows your numbers.

Chat with an AI coach about your game, get a daily tip, and follow practice plans built around whatever is costing you the most shots.

  • Answers that know your bag, your index and your last round
  • Personalised practice plans with drills and time estimates
  • A fresh tip from your coach every day
Apple Watch

Leave your phone in the bag.

The Apple Watch app measures distances on its own, with your caddie’s club pick, hazards and scoring on your wrist — plus a watch-face complication, and rounds saved to Apple Health as golf workouts.

Privacy

Built to respect your data.

  • Swing videos stay on your phoneSwing analysis runs on your device. Videos are never uploaded.
  • AI only with your permissionNothing is sent to our AI provider until you allow it, and you can turn it off at any time.
  • No ads, no trackingNo ad networks, no tracking across other apps, and we never sell your data.
