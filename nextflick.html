Features

Less scrolling. More watching.

Pick your favourite genres, actors and directors, and your suggestions get sharper the more you use the app.

  • The Director’s Chair — tune the taste signals behind your feed
  • The genre dial — love it, stay neutral, or never see it again
  • Follow the people whose work you love

JARVIS, the built-in AI assistant, takes plain-language requests — “thrillers like Se7en”, “underrated comedies”, “recent sci-fi with strong reviews” — and answers with mood and nuance.

  • Recommendations, insights and hidden gems
  • Actor and director information on demand

Browse trending, popular and upcoming titles, then narrow them down by media type, genre, release year and minimum rating.

Trailers, cast and reviews on every title — and which streaming services carry it, so you stop hopping between apps to find out.

Save anything you want to watch later and keep your films and shows organised in one searchable place.
