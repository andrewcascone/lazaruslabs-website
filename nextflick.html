NextFlick

Find your next watch, faster.

NextFlick is an all-in-one hub for discovering movies and TV shows that match your taste. Instead of scrolling through several streaming apps, you get personalised recommendations, an AI assistant that understands what you're in the mood for, a watchlist you can actually keep track of, and a clear answer to the only question that matters: where can I stream it?

iPhone & iPad · Android · Free · Requires iOS 13 or later

Download on the App Store ↗ Get it on Google Play ↗
Features

Less scrolling. More watching.

Recommendations that learn

Pick your favourite genres, actors and directors, and your suggestions get sharper the more you use the app.

JARVIS, your AI assistant

Ask in plain language — “thrillers like Se7en”, “underrated comedies”, “recent sci-fi with strong reviews” — and get answers that account for mood and nuance.

Where to stream it

Every title shows which streaming services carry it, so you stop hopping between apps to find out.

Library and watchlist

Save anything you want to watch later, and keep your films and shows organised in one place.

Discover, filtered your way

Browse trending, popular and upcoming titles, then narrow by media type, genre, release year and minimum rating.

The Director's Chair

Tune the taste signals behind your recommendations with a genre dial and your own lists of people to follow.
