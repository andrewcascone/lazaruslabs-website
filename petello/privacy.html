Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

This policy explains what data the Petello app for iPhone and Android collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We've written it to be read, not skimmed past.

The short version

  • Petello is built to work on your phone. Your pets' records stay there unless you turn on backup and sync, share a household, or use a feature that needs our servers.
  • AI requests go through our servers. OpenAI receives your question and the details about your pet that help answer it, but never your name, email address, account or location.
  • Photos and documents you ask the AI to read are analysed and not stored by us.
  • Your location is used only to find vets near you, once per search, and is never stored.
  • Premium has no ads. The free plan shows ads from Google AdMob, which are personalised only if you allow it.
  • We don't sell your personal data.
  • You can export your data or delete it, and your account, in the app at any time.

1. Who we are

Petello is published by Lazarus Labs (“we”, “us”, “our”), based in Ontario, Canada. We are responsible for the personal data described in this policy.

Questions about this policy: info@thelazaruslabs.com.

2. What we collect, and why

On your phone

What:

  • Pet profiles: name, species, breed, birthday, sex, colour, microchip and registration numbers, allergies, conditions, diet, your vet's details and photos.
  • Records: the health timeline (symptoms, observations, vet visits, vaccines, weights, notes and attachments), daily check-ins, medications and the doses you log, reminders, expenses, weight goals, records and documents you scan or import, and moments.
  • Companion conversations, weekly digests and visit briefs.
  • Your profile and preferences: display name, optional email address and profile photo, emergency contacts, and app settings.

Why: to give you the app. This information is stored in the app on your phone. We don't receive it unless you turn on backup and sync, share a household, use an AI feature (section 3), or share something yourself, such as a PDF report.

Account

What: your email address and name, or the name and email address that Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In provides.

Why: to create your account, keep it secure, back up and sync your pets if you choose to, and share a household.

Without an account: every installation gets an anonymous identifier, with no name or email address. We use it to apply daily limits to the AI features, connect your purchases to you, and protect our servers from abuse. If you later create an account, the identifier moves to it.

Backup and sync (optional)

What: when you turn on backup and sync, which needs an account, we store a copy of your pets and their records on our servers:

  • pet profiles and photos;
  • the health timeline and its attachments;
  • check-ins, medications and doses, reminders, expenses, goals and moments;
  • documents, including their page scans, which can contain your name, address and other personal details.

What stays on your phone: Companion conversations, weekly digests and visit briefs, and your app settings are never uploaded.

Why: to keep your pets safe if you lose or change your phone, keep them in sync across your devices, and share them with your household.

Files are uploaded as they are, so photos can include any information they contain, such as where they were taken.

Location

  • We use your location only when you search for vets or emergency hospitals near you, and only while the app is in use. We never use it in the background.
  • Your phone sends your location to our server, which rounds it to about 100 metres before searching Google Places. Your location is not stored, and the search results are not saved.
  • Your location is not sold and is not used for advertising by us.

Camera, photos and files

  • Pet photos, record attachments and documents are stored on your phone, and also on our servers if you turn on backup and sync.
  • Scanning a document happens on your phone.
  • Photos and documents you send to the AI features are analysed and not stored by us. See AI features and OpenAI.

Purchases

What: your plan, the product you bought, purchase, renewal and expiry dates, whether you're in a free trial or a billing grace period, and the store you bought it from.

Why: to unlock Premium on all your devices and for the features that run on our servers. Your payment card details are handled by Apple or Google and never reach us.

Usage counts

What: how many AI requests, vet searches and subscription checks you make each day.

Why: to apply your plan's daily limits and prevent abuse.

Diagnostics

What: crash and error reports, including the device model, operating system version, app version and technical logs. They contain no name, email address or account ID.

Why: to find and fix bugs.

App integrity

What: Firebase App Check uses Apple App Attest and DeviceCheck, or Google Play Integrity, to check that requests come from the genuine Petello app.

Why: to prevent abuse of our services.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. We don't use push notifications, and we don't send marketing notifications.

3. AI features and OpenAI

Petello Companion, photo analysis, smart document filing, and the weekly digest and visit brief are powered by OpenAI.

AI features run only when you use them: when you ask Companion a question, ask Petello to read a document, or create a digest or visit brief. Requests go through our servers, never directly from your phone to OpenAI. If you don't want data sent to OpenAI, don't use these features. Everything else in the app works without them.

What OpenAI receives when you use an AI feature

Only what each feature needs:

  • Companion:
    • your question, and up to 12 earlier messages from the same conversation;
    • the photos you attach (Premium), and anything visible in them.
    • Unless you turn off “Share pet context with Companion” in Profile → Companion & privacy, it also receives details that help answer care questions:
      • your pet's name, species, breed, age and sex, and whether they're spayed or neutered;
      • known allergies and conditions, diet notes and habitat;
      • active medication reminders, recent timeline entries and the next vet appointment.
  • Smart document filing (Premium):
    • the pages of the document you ask Petello to read, and anything on them. A vet record or invoice can show your name, address or phone number.
    • your pet's name and species, the type of record you filed it as, your country, and today's date.
  • Weekly digest and visit brief (Premium): your pet's name, species and age, and facts the app calculated from your records, such as “7 check-ins; appetite lower on 2 days”.

What OpenAI never receives

OpenAI never receives your name, email address, account ID or location. It also never receives your pet's microchip or registration number, your vet's details, your expenses, or your documents, unless you ask Petello to read a document.

How OpenAI uses it

OpenAI processes this data to generate responses, under its API terms. We ask OpenAI not to store responses, and data sent through the OpenAI API is not used to train OpenAI's models. OpenAI keeps abuse-monitoring logs of API requests for up to 30 days and then deletes them, unless it needs to keep them longer to comply with the law or to protect its services from harm.

What we keep

  • Companion conversations are saved only on your phone, even with backup and sync on. You can delete them at any time in Profile → Companion & privacy → Delete conversation history.
  • Weekly digests and visit briefs are saved only on your phone.
  • From smart document filing, only the details you confirm are saved, as records on your phone.
  • Photos and documents sent to the AI features are not stored by us.

4. Ads on the free plan

  • The free plan shows ads from Google AdMob. Premium has no ads, and on Premium the ad software isn't started at all.
  • Ads appear only on the Home screen, in the health timeline and on pet profiles. They never appear in Companion, on emergency screens, in reminders, during setup or in forms. We ask Google for ads suitable for general audiences.
  • AdMob may collect information from your device to show and measure ads, including your device's advertising identifier, IP address and how you interact with ads. Google describes this in How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices:

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and wherever else the law requires it, Google's consent message asks you before ads are shown. You can change your answer in Profile → Your data → Ad privacy choices.
  • On iPhone, we ask for permission to track before ads can use your advertising identifier. If you say no, you'll see ads that aren't personalised. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android, you can delete or reset your advertising ID in your device's settings.

We don't give advertisers your name, email address, pet records or location.

5. Household sharing

With Premium, you can invite people to your household with an invite code. Codes are single use and expire after 72 hours. We don't ask for, store or email the invitee's email address.

Roles:

  • Members can see and change everything about the pets you share: profiles, photos, the health timeline, check-ins, medications, reminders, expenses, goals, documents and moments.
  • Caregivers, such as a sitter or dog walker, can see and change the same, except expenses and documents.

Everyone in a household can see each other's display names, and who logged or changed each record (“Alex gave Bella her medication at 8:02”). They can't see your Companion conversations, digests or visit briefs, or your profile.

You can assign a reminder to one person; only their phone will notify them.

6. Service providers we share data with

These providers process data on our behalf to run Petello. We don't sell personal data.

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Crashlytics, App Check): your account and anonymous identifier, your backed-up pets, records and files, household membership, subscription status, usage counts and crash reports. Our database, file storage and server functions run in the United States (Iowa).
  • OpenAI: as described in AI features and OpenAI, only when you use an AI feature.
  • Google AdMob: on the free plan only, as described in Ads on the free plan.
  • RevenueCat: manages subscriptions. It receives your account ID or anonymous identifier (not your name or email address) and purchase and transaction information.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple and App Store payments.
  • Google: Google Sign-In, Google Play payments, and Google Places, which receives your rounded location, or the place you typed, when you search for vets.

When you choose to call a clinic, get directions, open a website or share a report, that app or website receives what it needs to do so, under its own privacy policy.

7. What we don't do

  • No selling or renting of personal data.
  • No ads on Premium.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites, except for ads on the free plan when you allow it.
  • No third-party analytics SDKs.
  • No location tracking in the background.
  • No marketing emails or notifications.
  • No uploading of your pets' records unless you turn on backup and sync, share a household, or use an AI feature.

8. Retention and deletion

On your phone. Your data stays on your phone until you delete it.

  • You can delete individual pets, records, documents and conversations at any time.
  • Profile → Your data → Delete all data on this device removes everything Petello has stored there.
  • Uninstalling the app also removes it.

On our servers:

  • Backup and sync. If you turn on backup and sync, we keep your backup for as long as it's on and your account exists. When you delete a record, it's removed from our servers within 30 days. You can turn backup and sync off and delete your cloud copy in Profile → Your data → Backup & sync.
  • Deleting your account (Profile → Account → Delete account) permanently deletes, from our servers:
    • your login;
    • your backed-up pets, records and files;
    • the invites you created;
    • your subscription status record and usage counts.
    It also removes you from any household you joined. Pets and records on your phone are kept unless you also delete them.
  • Households. If you delete your account, or a household you own, its shared pets are deleted from our servers for everyone in it, including records others added. If you leave a household or delete your account as a member, the records you added stay with the household.
  • Usage counts are deleted after a few days.

Kept after you delete your account:

  • your purchase history at RevenueCat and in the App Store or Google Play, which they need for billing and tax records. To ask us to delete your RevenueCat record, email support@thelazaruslabs.com;
  • our server logs, which can include your account ID or anonymous identifier, for up to 30 days;
  • crash reports, which Google Crashlytics keeps for 90 days, after which Google begins deleting them from its live and backup systems.

Without an account. Your anonymous identifier contains no personal information. If you never create an account, uninstalling the app deletes everything Petello stored on your phone, and the identifier can no longer be linked to you.

Subscriptions. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it first in your App Store account (on iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions) or Google Play account, or you will continue to be charged.

9. Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, by:

  • using the in-app controls:
    • your pet and profile screens;
    • Profile → Your data (Export my data, Backup & sync, Ad privacy choices and Delete all data on this device);
    • Profile → Account → Delete account;
    • your device's permission settings for location, camera, photos, notifications and tracking; or
  • emailing support@thelazaruslabs.com.

Your export is a JSON file with your pets and all their records, and your Companion conversations.

We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request. If you live in Canada, you can also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada. The sections below describe the additional rights you have if you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California.

10. EEA and UK residents (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, Lazarus Labs is the controller of your personal data.

Our legal bases for using your data:

  • Contract: to provide the app and the plan you signed up for:
    • your account, backup and sync, and household sharing;
    • the AI features you use;
    • your location when you search for vets;
    • purchases.
  • Consent: ads on the free plan where consent is required, including personalised ads, and the device permissions you grant (location, camera, photos, notifications and tracking). You can withdraw consent at any time; this doesn't affect processing that happened before.
  • Legitimate interests: crash diagnostics to fix bugs, and app integrity checks and usage limits to prevent abuse.

Your rights: you have the right to:

  • access your data;
  • correct it;
  • delete it;
  • restrict or object to how we use it;
  • receive it in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent.

To use these rights, use the in-app controls or email support@thelazaruslabs.com. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

International transfers: our database, file storage and server functions run in the United States, and some of our service providers, including OpenAI, Google and RevenueCat, may process your data in the United States or elsewhere. When your data is transferred out of the EEA or the UK, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK addendum to them, included in our providers' data processing terms.

11. California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

If you live in California, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act.

Categories of personal information we collect (described in section 2):

  • Identifiers: name, email address, account ID, anonymous identifier, and, on the free plan, your device's advertising identifier (collected by Google AdMob)
  • Commercial information: subscription and purchase records, and the pet expenses you back up
  • Geolocation: your location when you search for vets, which is used once and not stored
  • Photos and documents: pet photos, record attachments and documents you back up or share with your household
  • Internet or other electronic activity: interactions with ads on the free plan
  • Other information you provide: pet profiles and health records you back up or share with your household

Sale and sharing: we do not sell your personal information. On the free plan, if you allow personalised ads, Google AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier for cross-context behavioural advertising, which California law may treat as “sharing”. To opt out, do any of these:

  • decline tracking on iPhone (Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking);
  • delete your advertising ID on Android;
  • change your Ad privacy choices in the app;
  • use Premium, which has no ads.

We use sensitive personal information, such as your login and precise location, only to provide the features you use.

Your rights:

  • to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose
  • to delete your personal information
  • to correct inaccurate personal information
  • to opt out of the sharing of personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising
  • to limit the use of sensitive personal information (we already use it only to provide the service)
  • not to be discriminated against for using any of these rights

To make a request, use the in-app controls or email support@thelazaruslabs.com. You can also ask an authorised agent to make a request for you.

12. Children

Petello is not directed to children under 13, or under the minimum age required in your country if it is higher (in some parts of the EEA, that is 16). We don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact support@thelazaruslabs.com and we will remove it.

13. Security

  • Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS, and Google encrypts it at rest.
  • Our database and storage use access rules, so each account can reach only its own backup and the households it belongs to, and caregivers can't reach expenses or documents.
  • Requests to our AI and other services are checked on our servers, and our provider keys never leave them.

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

14. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date at the top. If a change is material, we'll also tell you in the app.
