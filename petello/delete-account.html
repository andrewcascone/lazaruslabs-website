Delete your Petello account

You can delete your Petello account, and the data stored with it on our servers, at any time. Petello is made by Lazarus Labs.

Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Petello and make sure you're signed in.
  2. Go to Profile → Account → Delete account.
  3. Confirm it's you (your password, or Sign in with Apple or Google).

Your account and its cloud data are deleted right away.

Ask us to delete it

If you no longer have the app, email support@thelazaruslabs.com from the email address on your Petello account, with the subject “Delete my Petello account”. We'll confirm the request is yours, then delete your account and its data within 30 days and let you know when it's done.

Before you delete

  • Cancel your subscription first. Deleting your account doesn't cancel Petello Premium. Cancel it in your App Store account (on iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions) or in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions), or you'll keep being charged.
  • Pets on your phone stay on your phone. Deleting your account removes your data from our servers. To clear your phone too, go to Profile → Your data → Delete all data on this device, or uninstall Petello.

What's deleted

From our servers:

  • your login (name, email address and account ID);
  • your backed-up pets, health records, documents, photos, expenses and other files;
  • the household invites you created;
  • your subscription status record and daily usage counts.

You're also removed from any household you joined. If you own a household, its shared pets are deleted for everyone in it, including records others added. If you're a member, the records you added stay with that household.

What's kept, and for how long

  • Purchase history at RevenueCat and in the App Store or Google Play, which they need for billing and tax records. To ask us to delete your RevenueCat record, email support@thelazaruslabs.com.
  • Server logs, which can include your account ID, for up to 30 days.
  • Crash reports, which Google Crashlytics keeps for 90 days.

Delete some data without deleting your account

  • Your cloud backup: Profile → Your data → Backup & sync → turn off backup and delete your cloud copy.
  • Companion conversations: Profile → Companion & privacy → Delete conversation history.
  • Individual pets, records and documents: delete them in the app. Deleted records leave our servers within 30 days.
