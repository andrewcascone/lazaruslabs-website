1. The service

Petello is a pet health and care app for iPhone and Android (the “app” or the “service”), provided by Lazarus Labs. It helps you:

  • keep your pets' health records;
  • manage care routines, medications and vaccines;
  • track trends and expenses;
  • create reports;
  • find vets nearby;
  • get general guidance from an AI Companion.

How we handle your data is described in our Privacy Policy.

5. Not veterinary advice

  • Petello is not a veterinarian and does not provide veterinary diagnosis, treatment or prescriptions. Nothing in the app replaces an examination by a qualified veterinarian.
  • In an emergency, contact a veterinarian or emergency animal hospital immediately. Don't wait for the app, and don't rely on it to decide whether your pet needs care.
  • The emergency hub, warning signs and toxin reference are general information and can't cover every situation. Animal poison control lines are run by independent organisations and may charge a fee.
  • Always follow your veterinarian's instructions for medications, doses, vaccines and preventives. Petello shows what you or your vet entered; it doesn't check that a dose or schedule is right for your pet.

  • Smart document filing can misread names, dates, doses or values. You review what it found before anything is saved, and you're responsible for checking it.
  • If Companion, a digest or your pet's condition suggests something urgent, contact a veterinarian.
