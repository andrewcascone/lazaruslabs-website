Petello

Everything your pet needs. All in one place.

Petello keeps your pet's health, care and records together. Log symptoms, weights and vet visits, never miss a dose or a vaccine, see how they're really doing over time, and ask an AI Companion that knows your pet, with facts kept apart from possibilities and clear advice on when to call your vet.

iPhone · Android · Free, with Premium

Features

Everything your pet needs. All in one place.

Health timeline

Symptoms, vet visits, vaccines, weights and notes in one timeline, each labelled by where it came from: you, your vet or a record.

Daily check-ins

A ten-second check-in for appetite, energy, mood, stool and water, made for each species, with streaks and trends that build a picture your vet can use.

Care routines

Feeding, walks, grooming, cleaning and medication reminders suggested for each species. Tick them off right from the notification.

Medications

Doses exactly as prescribed, supply left, low-supply and refill alerts, and how consistently each dose was given.

Vaccines and preventives

Up to date, due soon or overdue at a glance, with reminders from the dates your vet gives you.

Trends

Weight with goals, wellness, symptoms, care consistency and spending, drawn from your own records.

Petello Companion

An AI pet-care assistant that knows your pet's records. It keeps facts apart from possibilities, tells you when something needs a vet now, and never diagnoses. With Premium, show it a photo of what you're seeing.

Records and smart filing

Scan or import vet records, lab results, certificates and invoices. With Premium, Petello reads them into timeline entries, reminders and expenses, and you check every detail first.

Vet-ready reports

A polished PDF of history, medications, vaccines and trends for any visit, plus a visit brief with questions to ask. A one-page summary, a sitter care sheet and an emergency card are free.

Weekly digest

A plain-language summary of the week from what you logged, and what's worth watching.

Emergency hub

Call your vet, find an emergency hospital that's open now, reach animal poison control, and check the warning signs that mean “go now”.

Lost-pet mode

A print-ready poster with tear-off tabs and a scan-to-call code, plus a checklist of what to do first.

Find a vet

Clinics and emergency hospitals near you, with hours, phone numbers and directions.

Expenses

Vet bills, food, insurance and supplies by pet and by month.

Household sharing

Invite family and sitters so everyone follows the same routines and sees who fed, walked or medicated, with roles for family members and caregivers.

Every kind of pet

Dogs, cats, birds, rabbits, hamsters, guinea pigs, ferrets, reptiles, fish, horses and more, each with care suggestions made for them.

Privacy

Your pet's records, on your terms.

  • Records stay on your phonePetello works without an account. Your pets' records leave your phone only if you turn on backup and sync, or use a feature that needs it.
  • Photos aren't storedPhotos and documents you ask Petello's AI to read are analysed, then discarded.
  • Export or delete anytimeTake a copy of your data with you, or delete it from inside the app.
