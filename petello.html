Petello

Everything your pet needs. All in one place.

Log symptoms, weights and vet visits, never miss a dose or a vaccine, see how they’re really doing over time, and ask an AI Companion that knows your pet.

Features

Care, health and records, together.

Symptoms, vet visits, vaccines, weights and notes in one timeline, each labelled by where it came from: you, your vet or a record.

  • Daily check-ins

    Ten seconds for appetite, energy, mood, stool and water, made for each species, with streaks and trends your vet can use.

  • Vaccines and preventives

    Up to date, due soon or overdue at a glance, with reminders from the dates your vet gives you.

  • Trends and spending

    Weight with goals, wellness, symptoms and care consistency — plus vet bills, food, insurance and supplies by pet and by month.

Feeding, walks, grooming, cleaning and medication reminders, suggested for each species. Tick them off right from the notification.

  • Medications

    Doses exactly as prescribed, supply left, refill alerts, and how consistently each dose was given.

  • Household sharing

    Invite family and sitters so everyone follows the same routines and sees who fed, walked or medicated.

  • Every kind of pet

    Dogs, cats, birds, rabbits, reptiles, fish, horses and more, each with care suggestions made for them.

Ask about your pet and get answers that use their records, keep facts apart from possibilities, and tell you clearly when something needs a vet now. It never diagnoses — and with Premium, you can show it a photo of what you’re seeing.

  • Records and smart filing

    Scan vet records, lab results and invoices. With Premium, Petello reads them into entries, reminders and expenses — and you check every detail first.

  • Weekly digest

    A plain-language summary of the week from what you logged, and what’s worth watching.

A polished PDF of history, medications, vaccines and trends for any visit, plus a visit brief with questions to ask. A one-page summary, a sitter care sheet and an emergency card are free.

  • Emergency hub

    Call your vet, find an emergency hospital that’s open now, reach animal poison control, and check the signs that mean “go now”.

  • Lost-pet mode

    A print-ready poster with tear-off tabs and a scan-to-call code, plus a checklist of what to do first.

  • Find a vet

    Clinics and emergency hospitals near you, with hours, phone numbers and directions.

Privacy

Your pet’s records, on your terms.

On your phone

Records stay on your phone

Petello works without an account. Your pets’ records leave your phone only if you turn on backup and sync, or use a feature that needs it.

Privacy

Photos aren’t stored

Photos and documents you ask Petello’s AI to read are analysed, then discarded.

Your data

Export or delete anytime

Take a copy of your data with you, or delete it from inside the app.
