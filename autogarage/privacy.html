Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

This policy explains what data the AutoGarage app for iPhone and Android collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We've written it to be read, not skimmed past.

The short version

  • AI requests go through our servers. OpenAI receives your question, your photos and details about your car, but never your name, email address, account or location.
  • Photos you send to the AI tools are analysed and not stored, unless you attach a scanned receipt to a service record.
  • Your location is used only to find mechanics near you, once per search, and is never stored.
  • Plus and Pro have no ads. The free plan shows ads from Google AdMob, which are personalised only if you allow it.
  • We don't sell your personal data.
  • You can export your data or delete your account in the app at any time.

1. Who we are

AutoGarage is published by Lazarus Labs (“we”, “us”, “our”), based in Ontario, Canada. We are responsible for the personal data described in this policy.

Questions about this policy: info@thelazaruslabs.com.

2. What we collect, and why

Account

What: your name and email address. If you use Google Sign-In, or Sign in with Apple on iPhone, the name and email address they provide.

Why: to create your account, keep it secure, and keep your garage in sync across your devices.

Without an account: if you choose Skip for now, we create an anonymous account for you, with no name or email address. Your garage is stored on our servers in the same way as any other account, but only the phone you created it on can reach it. If you later create an account, your garage moves to it.

Profile and preferences

What: your display name, a profile photo or preset badge (optional), distance units, currency, notification preferences, and your driving habits, such as your daily commute and how many days a week you drive.

Why: to personalise the app, schedule your reminders, and project mileage and fuel costs.

Your vehicles

What:

  • Vehicle details: year, make, model, trim, engine, transmission, drivetrain, fuel type, body type, paint colour, VIN, nickname and photos.
  • Ownership details: odometer readings, purchase date, price and mileage. If you add them, loan or lease details (such as the monthly payment, term and interest rate) and the details used for market value (such as condition, accident history, title status, number of owners and modifications).
  • Records: service records (date, mileage, work done, parts, costs, shop name, notes and attachments), expenses, fuel fill-ups (including the station, if you enter one), reminders and custom maintenance intervals.
  • Glovebox documents: the documents you add, such as ownership, registration and insurance, with their type, title, number (for example, a policy number), issuer, dates, notes and page scans. These documents can contain your name, address and other personal details.

Why: to keep your vehicle records, forecast maintenance, calculate costs, estimate market value, send renewal reminders, and create the PDF reports you ask for.

OBD-II scans

  • When you scan with an OBD-II adapter, we save the result to your car's history: trouble codes, the check-engine light status, emissions readiness, readings such as battery voltage, engine speed, coolant temperature, fuel level and fuel trims, freeze-frame data (which can include the car's speed when a fault was recorded), the odometer, the VIN, and the adapter's name and protocol.
  • Live gauges are shown on screen only and are not saved. Demo car scans are never saved.
  • The adapter you last used is remembered on your phone so you can reconnect in one tap.

Why: to explain trouble codes, track your car's health over time, and keep your odometer up to date.

Location

  • We use your location only when you search for nearby mechanics, and only while the app is in use. We never use it in the background.
  • Your phone sends your location to our server, which rounds it to about 100 metres before searching Google Places. Your location is not stored, and the search results are not saved.
  • Your location is not sold and is not used for advertising by us.

Camera, photos and files

  • VIN barcode scanning happens on your phone. The camera image is not uploaded.
  • Car photos, profile photos, service attachments and Glovebox pages are uploaded to our storage. Files are uploaded as they are, so photos can include any information they contain, such as where they were taken.
  • Photos for the AI tools (warning lights, symptoms, receipts, repair quotes and tires) are analysed and not stored. See AI features and OpenAI. If you save a scanned receipt as a service record, its pages are attached to that record and stored with it.

Purchases

What: your plan, the product you bought, purchase, renewal and expiry dates, and the store you bought it from.

Why: to unlock the plan you paid for, on all your devices. Your payment card details are handled by Apple or Google and never reach us.

Usage counts

What: how many AI requests, mechanic searches and sharing invites you make each day, and records of the rewarded ads you've watched on the free plan.

Why: to apply your plan's daily limits, unlock features you earned by watching an ad, and prevent abuse.

Diagnostics

What: crash and error reports, including the device model, operating system version, app version and technical logs. They contain no name, email address or user ID.

Why: to find and fix bugs.

App integrity

What: Firebase App Check uses Apple App Attest and DeviceCheck, or Google Play Integrity, to check that requests come from the genuine AutoGarage app.

Why: to prevent abuse of our services.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. We don't use push notifications, and we don't send marketing notifications.

3. AI features and OpenAI

AutoGarage AI (chat, warning lights, symptom diagnosis, receipt scanning, quote checks and tire checks), market value estimates and parts advice are powered by OpenAI.

AI features run only when you use them: when you ask a question, use a tool or request a market value. Requests go through our servers, never directly from your phone to OpenAI. If you don't want data sent to OpenAI, don't use these features. Everything else in the app works without them.

What OpenAI receives when you use an AI feature

Only what each feature needs:

  • your question, and up to 12 earlier messages from the same conversation
  • details about the car you're asking about: year, make, model, trim, engine, transmission, drivetrain, fuel type and age
  • its mileage, recent service history (including notes you added to service records), overdue maintenance, driving habits, fuel and cost totals, and a summary of its most recent OBD-II scan
  • the photos you take or choose for an AI tool, and anything visible in them. A receipt or quote can show your name, address or licence plate.
  • for market value and parts advice, only the car's details and mileage. Market value may also use OpenAI's web search to check current prices.

What OpenAI never receives

OpenAI never receives your name, email address, account ID or location, or your car's VIN, nickname, purchase price, loan or lease details, or Glovebox documents.

How OpenAI uses it

OpenAI processes this data to generate responses, under its API terms. We ask OpenAI not to store responses, and data sent through the OpenAI API is not used to train OpenAI's models. OpenAI keeps abuse-monitoring logs of API requests for up to 30 days and then deletes them, unless it needs to keep them longer to comply with the law or to protect its services from harm.

What we keep

  • AI conversations are saved to your account so you can come back to them. You can delete a conversation at any time.
  • Market value estimates are saved with the car.
  • AI tool results are not saved, unless you continue one as a conversation.
  • Photos sent to the AI tools are not stored by us.

4. Ads on the free plan

  • The free plan shows ads from Google AdMob. Plus and Pro have no ads, and on those plans the ad software isn't started at all.
  • AdMob may collect information from your device to show and measure ads, including your device's advertising identifier, IP address and how you interact with ads. Google describes this in How Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Your choices:
    • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and wherever else the law requires it, Google's consent message asks you before ads are shown. You can change your answer in Profile → Privacy, export & deletion → Ad privacy choices.
    • On iPhone, we ask for permission to track before ads can use your advertising identifier. If you say no, you'll see ads that aren't personalised. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
    • On Android, you can delete or reset your advertising ID in your device's settings.
  • Rewarded ads: if you choose to watch an ad to open a feature for 24 hours, the unlock is saved on your phone. The app also gives Google your account ID and the feature with the ad, so Google can confirm the reward to our server. We keep a record of each confirmed reward (your account ID, the feature and the time) to prevent reward fraud.
  • We don't give advertisers your name, email address, vehicle records or location.

5. Family sharing

  • On AutoGarage Pro, you can share a car with others as an editor or a viewer by giving them an invite code.
  • Everyone you share a car with can see everything about that car, including its VIN, photos, purchase price, loan or lease details, service and fuel records, expenses, reminders, Glovebox documents, OBD-II scans and market value estimates. Editors can also add, change and delete its records.
  • People sharing a car can see each other's display names and profile photos. If you haven't set a display name, the part of your email address before the @ is shown instead.
  • They can't see your profile, your AI conversations or your other cars.
  • When you create an invite, you can add the person's email address as a note for yourself. We store it with the invite, but we don't send email to it.

6. Service providers we share data with

These providers process data on our behalf to run AutoGarage. We don't sell personal data.

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Crashlytics, App Check): your account, profile, vehicle data, uploaded files, AI conversations, subscription status, usage counts and crash reports. Our database and server functions run in Canada (Montréal).
  • OpenAI: as described in AI features and OpenAI, only when you use an AI feature.
  • Google AdMob: on the free plan only, as described in Ads on the free plan.
  • RevenueCat: manages subscriptions. It receives your account ID (not your name or email address) and purchase and transaction information.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple and App Store payments.
  • Google: Google Sign-In, Google Play payments, and Google Places, which receives your rounded location when you search for mechanics.
  • US National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA): receives the VIN you enter or scan, to decode your car's details.
  • US EPA (fueleconomy.gov): receives a car's year, make and model, to look up its official specs and fuel economy. No personal data is sent.

When you choose to get directions, call a shop or open its website, that app or website receives what it needs to do so, under its own privacy policy.

7. What we don't do

  • No selling or renting of personal data.
  • No ads on Plus or Pro.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites, except for ads on the free plan when you allow it.
  • No third-party analytics SDKs.
  • No location tracking in the background.
  • No marketing emails or notifications.

8. Retention and deletion

  • We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. You can delete individual cars, records, documents and AI conversations at any time.
  • You can delete your account in the app: Profile → Privacy, export & deletion → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your profile, cars and all their records, Glovebox documents, OBD-II scans, market value estimates, AI conversations, usage counts, uploaded files, invites, and your login itself. It also removes you from any cars shared with you.
  • Shared cars: if you delete a car you own, or your account, the car is deleted for everyone you shared it with, including records they added. If you leave a shared car or delete your account, the records you added stay with the owner's car.
  • A few things are kept after you delete your account:
    • records of rewarded ads you watched (your former account ID, the feature and the time), to prevent reward fraud;
    • your purchase history at RevenueCat and in the App Store or Google Play, which they need for billing and tax records. To ask us to delete your RevenueCat record, email support@thelazaruslabs.com;
    • our server logs, which can include your account ID, for a limited period.
  • Guest garages: if you sign out of a garage you created with Skip for now, without deleting it first, you can no longer reach it. Its data stays on our servers, but it is no longer linked to you, and we can't identify it for you. To erase a guest garage, delete it from Profile before you sign out.
  • When you replace your profile photo, the old one is kept until you delete your account.
  • Crash reports are kept by Google Crashlytics for 90 days, after which Google begins deleting them from its live and backup systems.
  • Data stored only on your phone, including cached copies of your garage, is removed when you uninstall the app.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it first in your App Store account (on iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions) or Google Play account, or you will continue to be charged.

9. Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, by:

  • using the in-app controls: your profile and vehicle screens, Profile → Privacy, export & deletion (Export my data, Ad privacy choices and Delete my account), and your device's permission settings for location, camera, Bluetooth, notifications and tracking; or
  • emailing support@thelazaruslabs.com.

Your export is a JSON file with your profile, plan, the cars you own and their records, and your AI conversations.

We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request. If you live in Canada, you can also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada. The sections below describe the additional rights you have if you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California.

10. EEA and UK residents (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, Lazarus Labs is the controller of your personal data.

Our legal bases for using your data:

  • Contract: to provide the app and the plan you signed up for: your account, profile, vehicle data, OBD-II scans, family sharing, the AI features you use, your location when you search for mechanics, and purchases.
  • Consent: ads on the free plan where consent is required, including personalised ads, and the device permissions you grant (location, camera, Bluetooth, local network, notifications and tracking). You can withdraw consent at any time; this doesn't affect processing that happened before.
  • Legitimate interests: crash diagnostics to fix bugs, and app integrity checks, usage limits and reward records to prevent abuse.

Your rights: you have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent. To use these rights, use the in-app controls or email support@thelazaruslabs.com. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

International transfers: our database and server functions run in Canada, which the European Commission and the UK recognise as providing adequate protection for commercial organisations. Some of our service providers, including OpenAI, Google and RevenueCat, may process your data in the United States or elsewhere. Where this happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

If you live in California, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act.

Categories of personal information we collect (described in section 2):

  • Identifiers: name, email address, account ID, and, on the free plan, your device's advertising identifier (collected by Google AdMob)
  • Commercial information: subscription and purchase records, and the vehicle purchase, finance and expense details you enter
  • Geolocation: your location when you search for mechanics, which is used once and not stored
  • Photos and documents: car and profile photos, service attachments and Glovebox documents
  • Internet or other electronic activity: interactions with ads on the free plan
  • Other information you provide: vehicle details and VINs, service, fuel and OBD-II records, and AI conversations

Sale and sharing: we do not sell your personal information. On the free plan, if you allow personalised ads, Google AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier for cross-context behavioural advertising, which California law may treat as “sharing”. To opt out, decline tracking on iPhone (Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking), delete your advertising ID on Android, change your Ad privacy choices in the app, or use Plus or Pro, which have no ads. We use sensitive personal information, such as your login and precise location, only to provide the features you use.

Your rights:

  • to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose
  • to delete your personal information
  • to correct inaccurate personal information
  • to opt out of the sharing of personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising
  • to limit the use of sensitive personal information (we already use it only to provide the service)
  • not to be discriminated against for using any of these rights

To make a request, use the in-app controls or email support@thelazaruslabs.com. You can also ask an authorised agent to make a request for you.

12. Children

AutoGarage is not directed to children under 13, or under the minimum age required in your country if it is higher (in some parts of the EEA, that is 16). We don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact support@thelazaruslabs.com and we will remove it.

13. Security

  • Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS, and Google encrypts it at rest.
  • Our database and storage use per-user access rules, so each account can reach only its own data, plus the cars that have been shared with it.
  • Requests to our AI and other services are checked on our servers, and our provider keys never leave them.

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

When we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date at the top. If a change is material, we'll also tell you in the app.
