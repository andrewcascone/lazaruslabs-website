7. OBD-II diagnostics

  • OBD-II adapters are made and sold by third parties. We don't make, sell or support them, and we can't guarantee that a particular adapter or vehicle will work with the app.
  • Use an adapter as its maker instructs. Make sure it doesn't interfere with your pedals or controls, and unplug it if your vehicle behaves unusually.
  • Clearing trouble codes turns off the check-engine light but doesn't fix the underlying problem. It also resets your vehicle's emissions readiness monitors, so the vehicle may fail an emissions inspection until it has been driven enough for them to complete. You clear codes at your own risk.
  • Code descriptions, severity ratings and advice are general information and may not match your vehicle exactly.
