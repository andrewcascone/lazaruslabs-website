Frequently asked questions

Restore purchases
  1. In Profile, tap your plan card.
  2. Tap Restore at the top right. (On a paid plan, tap Restore purchases instead.)

Make sure you're signed in to AutoGarage, and to the App Store or Google Play with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to subscribe. Your plan belongs to your AutoGarage account, so it also works on your other devices when you sign in.

Cancel or change a subscription

In the app: in Profile, tap your plan card, then Manage or cancel.

On iPhone:

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  3. Tap AutoGarage, then choose a different plan or cancel.

On Android: open the Play Store, tap your profile icon, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Upgrades from Plus to Pro apply straight away. Downgrades apply at the end of the current period. If you subscribed on another device, manage it in the store you bought it from.

Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a subscription.

Using AutoGarage without an account

If you chose Skip for now when you first opened the app, your garage is tied to this phone only. Paid plans need an account.

To keep your garage and use it on other devices:

  1. In Profile, tap the No account yet card.
  2. Tap Create account and keep my garage, or continue with Google or Apple.

Everything you've logged moves to your new account. If you sign out without creating an account, your garage is lost.

Adding a car
  1. In the Garage tab, tap +.
  2. On the VIN tab, type your VIN and tap Look up vehicle, or tap the scan icon to scan the VIN barcode on the driver's door jamb or at the base of the windshield.
  3. Or, on the Make & model tab, pick the year, make, model, and engine and transmission. If your car isn't listed, tap Can't find it? to enter the details yourself.
  4. Enter the current odometer reading and tap Add to garage.

The Free plan includes 1 car, Plus includes 3, and Pro is unlimited. Cars shared with you by someone else don't count towards your plan.

Connecting an OBD-II adapter

OBD-II diagnostics are part of AutoGarage Pro. AutoGarage works with ELM327-compatible Bluetooth LE adapters (such as Vgate iCar, OBDLink CX and Veepeak BLE) and Wi-Fi adapters. Older “classic” Bluetooth adapters don't work.

  1. Plug the adapter into the OBD-II port under the dashboard and turn the ignition on.
  2. In Profile, tap OBD-II adapter (or, in the Service tab, tap Scan with OBD-II), then Connect adapter.
  3. Bluetooth: allow Bluetooth when asked, then tap your adapter in the list.
  4. Wi-Fi: open your phone's Settings → Wi-Fi and join the adapter's network (often “WiFi_OBDII” or “V-LINK”). Come back to AutoGarage, open the Wi-Fi tab and tap Connect. Most adapters use the address 192.168.0.10 and port 35000, which are filled in for you.

No adapter yet? Tap Try the demo car to see how it works. Anyone can use it, and demo scans aren't saved.

Clearing codes turns off the check-engine light, but it doesn't fix anything. If the fault is still there, the light comes back. It also resets the emissions monitors, so the car won't pass an emissions inspection until it has been driven for a few days.

My OBD-II adapter won't connect
  • Check the adapter is pushed fully into the port and its light is on.
  • Turn the ignition on. The engine running is best, and many adapters sleep without it.
  • Close any other OBD apps. An adapter can talk to only one app at a time.
  • Unplug the adapter for a few seconds, then try again.
  • Bluetooth: make sure Bluetooth is on and AutoGarage is allowed to use it. On iPhone, that's Settings → AutoGarage → Bluetooth. On Android, allow Nearby devices for AutoGarage. Only Bluetooth LE adapters appear in the list.
  • Wi-Fi: join the adapter's Wi-Fi network in Settings before connecting. On iPhone, also allow Local Network access for AutoGarage.
Sharing a car with family

Sharing a car needs AutoGarage Pro. The people you invite don't need a paid plan.

To invite someone:

  1. In the Garage tab, tap ⋯ on the car, then Sharing.
  2. Choose Editor (can log services, fuel, costs, documents and mileage) or Viewer (can see everything, but can't change anything).
  3. Tap Create invite code, then Share invite or Copy code.

Each code works once and expires after 7 days. Up to 8 people can share a car.

To join: in the Garage tab (or in Profile), tap Join a shared garage, enter the code and tap Join.

To change a role or remove someone: open Sharing for the car and tap ⋯ next to their name. To leave a car someone shared with you, open Sharing for it and tap Leave this car.

Reminders aren't arriving
  1. In Profile, tap Notifications and check that Allow notifications and the categories you want are on.
  2. If AutoGarage says notifications are turned off in Settings, tap Open Settings and allow them.

Maintenance dates are estimated from your mileage, so reminders move as you log odometer readings. Reminders are scheduled on your phone, so signing out turns them off on that phone.

Nearby mechanics needs my location

In the Service tab, tap Find a trusted mechanic. AutoGarage uses your location once for each search and doesn't store it.

If you see Where are you?, allow location access for AutoGarage in your phone's Settings, or tap Search in Maps instead.

I've reached today's AI limit

AutoGarage AI has a daily limit for each kind of request, which resets at midnight UTC. Plus and Pro have much higher limits than the Free plan. Diagnosing a symptom, scanning a receipt, checking a quote and checking tires are included with Plus. Identifying a warning light is free.

AI guidance can be wrong. For anything that affects safety, have the vehicle inspected.

Ads and ad privacy choices
  • Only the Free plan shows ads. Plus and Pro are ad-free. Tap Remove ads on any sponsored card to see the plans.
  • On the Free plan, you can watch a short ad to open the longer maintenance forecasts or cost analytics for 24 hours.
  • If you were asked for ad consent (for example, in Europe or the UK), you can change your answer in Profile → Privacy, export & deletion → Ad privacy choices.
  • On iPhone, you can change whether AutoGarage may track you for ads in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
Export my data
  1. In Profile, tap Privacy, export & deletion.
  2. Tap Export my data, then save or share the file.

You get a JSON file with your profile, plan, the cars you own and their records, and your AI conversations. For a PDF report you can give a buyer, tap ⋯ on a car in the Garage tab, then Export report (Pro).

Delete my account
  1. In Profile, tap Privacy, export & deletion.
  2. Tap Delete my account, type DELETE, and tap Delete account.

This permanently deletes your account, vehicles, service history, expenses, photos and AI conversations, and removes you from any cars shared with you. It doesn't cancel a subscription, so cancel that first (see above).

Privacy questions or a data request

Read our Privacy Policy. To access, correct, export or delete your data, use the controls in Profile → Privacy, export & deletion, or email support@thelazaruslabs.com.
