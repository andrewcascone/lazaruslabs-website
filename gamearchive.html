GameArchive+

Your gaming companion.

GameArchive+ brings your gaming world together in one place. Set the platforms, genres and game modes you care about, and the whole app reshapes around them — personalised recommendations, a discovery page built for real searching, and a library that tracks what you've played, what you're playing and what's next.

iPhone & iPad · Android · Free · Requires iOS 13 or later

Download on the App Store ↗ Get it on Google Play ↗
Features

Find your next game. Keep track of the rest.

Built around your platforms

Choose your platforms, genres and game modes, and discovery is shaped to match — no filtering through games you can't play.

GLaDOS, your AI assistant

Ask for recommendations, walkthrough guidance, lore explanations, or straight answers on bosses, quests and crafting.

Your own library

Track what you've played, what you're playing and what you want to get to later, all in one collection.

Discovery that goes deep

Advanced search with platform, genre and game-mode filters, plus trending titles and popular new releases.

Detailed game pages

Ratings, developer, release date and the platforms a game is on — the essentials, without the noise.

Tune it any time

A dashboard for your platforms, genres and game modes, so your recommendations move with your taste.
