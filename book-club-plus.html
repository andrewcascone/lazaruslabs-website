Book Club+

Find your next great read.

Features

Your reading life, beautifully organised.

A Home feed built around your taste — For You, Your Next Read, new releases in your favourite genres and more from authors you love. Every pick tells you why it fits.

  • Pick My Next Book — one standout pick at a time
  • Narrow it by genre, rating, length or mood
  • Start reading it, pass on it, or see another

Tell the assistant a book you loved, the feeling you’re after or how much time you have, and get real books you can open and save.

  • Refine as you go — “shorter”, “something darker”, “more like the first one”
  • Every suggestion opens the full book page
  • Save straight to Want to Read

Search by title, author or ISBN, explore genres and featured shelves, and see what other readers are adding, finishing and loving.

  • Filter by year, length, language and rating
  • Genres and featured shelves to wander through
  • New and noteworthy releases

Want to Read, Reading and Read shelves plus Favourites, with your progress, ratings and notes in one place.

  • Track progress by page or percentage
  • Rate in half stars
  • Private notes, quotes and questions for your book club

Set a yearly goal and see whether you’re on schedule, with stats that make every finished book count.

  • Books and pages read, and your average rating
  • Monthly activity and favourite genres
  • Optional daily reading reminders
