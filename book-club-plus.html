Book Club+

Find your next great read.

Book Club+ learns what you love from your shelves, ratings and favourite authors, then recommends books picked just for you. Ask the AI reading assistant for exactly the kind of book you're in the mood for, track your progress, and watch your reading life take shape. No account needed to start.

iPhone & iPad · Android · Free, with a one-time Remove Ads option

See features See screenshots
Features

Every book, from “want to read” to “read”.

Personal recommendations

A Home feed built around your taste — For You, Your Next Read, new releases in your favourite genres and more from authors you love. Every pick explains why it fits you.

AI reading assistant

Describe a mood, a book you loved or how much time you have, and get real books you can open and save. Refine as you go — “shorter”, “something darker”, “more like the first one”.

Pick My Next Book

Can't decide? Get one standout pick at a time, narrowed by genre, rating, length or mood. Start reading it, pass on it, or see another.

Discover

Search by title, author or ISBN, browse genres and featured shelves, filter by year, length, language and rating, and see what other readers are adding, finishing and loving.

Your library

Want to Read, Reading and Read shelves plus Favourites. Track progress by page or percentage, rate in half stars, and keep private notes, quotes and questions for your book club.

Reading stats & goals

Set a yearly goal and see whether you're on schedule. Books and pages read, average rating, monthly activity, favourite genres, and optional daily reminders.

Free, with an ad-free option

No subscription. Ever.

Book Club+ is free and supported by ads. Remove Ads is a single, one-time purchase, and you can restore it on your other devices.

Start as a guest, then create a free account with Apple, Google or email to keep your library on all your devices. Read the full privacy policy.
